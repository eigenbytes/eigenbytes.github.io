About

What is Eigenbytes?

Eigenbytes.blog is a practical hub for AI, Data Science, ML, and AI Security. We publish plain-English paper breakdowns, hands-on tutorials, and curated videos—organized for beginners through researchers.

Paper Reviews Hands-on Guides LLM Safety Community

Mission

We distill frontier research into actionable insights and reproducible workflows. Our bias is toward building: every post aims to help you ship something—faster, safer, and with a deeper understanding of why it works.

  • Clarity: Jargon-lite explanations without dumbing things down.
  • Rigor: Citations, experiments, and transparent assumptions.
  • Security-first: Practical guardrails for modern AI systems.

What We Publish

  • Paper Dives: digestible summaries + diagrams
  • Tutorials: end-to-end guides with code & data
  • Videos: curated talks and explainers
  • Community: skill-tiered Q&A boards

Our Values

Learn by Shipping

We turn concepts into working demos—so you can adapt them for your own stack.

Beginner-Safe, Expert-Tough

Clear entry points with depth for advanced readers: we annotate tradeoffs and edge cases.

Security & Safety

LLM policy firewalls, evals, PII hygiene, and abuse resistance are first-class topics—not afterthoughts.

Open & Friendly

We encourage questions and celebrate learning out loud. No gatekeeping.

How We Work

  1. Read broadly: conference papers, docs, and battle-tested blog posts.
  2. Prototype fast: minimal reproducible examples with diagrams.
  3. Stress test: edge cases, ablations, and safety checks.
  4. Document: what worked, what didn’t, and why.

Credits & Attributions

We link to original papers, repos, and datasets. If we missed an attribution, tell us and we’ll fix it quickly.

Want to contribute? Pitch a post, tutorial, or video curation idea.
